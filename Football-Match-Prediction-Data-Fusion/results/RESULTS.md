### Joining the two sources

- Kaggle matches in the five leagues: 14,585; clubs: 164, of which 80 are spelled differently in the Big Five file.
- Clubs matched by fixtures: 164 of 164 (name clashes: 0). Every pair is in name_matching.csv with its votes and string similarity.
- Matches joined: 14,585 (100.0%); the two sources agree on the score in 100.00% of them.
- Matching on spelling alone (closest normalised name in the same league) would have paired 3 clubs wrongly: Inter with Udinese Calcio instead of FC Internazionale Milano; Reggio Calabria with Cagliari Calcio instead of Urbs Reggina; Siena with Cesena instead of SS Robur Siena.
- Matches with odds and at least 9 rated starters per side: 14,571.
